Privacy / Data Rule

YouthLink will never infer LGBT/SOGIESC identity from sex assigned at birth, name, appearance, or other profile fields. If the SK later uses a lawful, voluntary and privacy-reviewed SOGIESC field, it can be added as a separate optional survey item and counted only from self-reported responses.
