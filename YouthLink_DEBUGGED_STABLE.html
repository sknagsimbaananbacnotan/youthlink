Privacy / Data Rule

YouthLink will never infer LGBT/SOGIESC identity from sex assigned at birth, name, appearance, or other profile fields. If the SK later uses a lawful, voluntary and privacy-reviewed SOGIESC field, it can be added as a separate optional survey item and counted only from self-reported responses.

YouthLink will flag the plan when total PPA appropriations are above the official annual budget. Disbursements cannot exceed the remaining appropriation of the selected PPA. This module is an internal monitoring ledger; official accounting, procurement, bank and audit records remain controlling.

About Us

YouthLink is the centralized youth information platform of the Office of the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Nagsimbaanan. It supports organized youth profiling, demographics, attendance, participation records, official communication, and reporting to help strengthen youth governance and improve programs and services for the Katipunan ng Kabataan.
